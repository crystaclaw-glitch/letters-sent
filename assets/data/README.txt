=========================
LETTER 1
=========================

**My Dearest,**

How have you been?

I've been wondering how to tell you that I miss you. 
After what happened, I know I shouldn't have said 
those things. I'm sorry. I thought keeping my 
distance would protect us both, even if it meant 
hurting quietly instead.

I still miss you.

Now all I have are the letters we once wrote, 
carefully kept all this time. Sometimes I wonder...

Who gets to see your lovely smile now?

Yours,
Me.


=========================
LETTER 2
=========================

My Dearest,

I hope you're keeping well.

You seem rather tired these days. Perhaps 
I'm mistaken, or perhaps you simply no longer 
wish to speak to me. Whatever it may be, I 
hope you'll tell me.

I may not be able to solve every problem you 
carry, but I can promise you this—you'll never 
have to carry them alone. If you have a thousand 
worries, then let me be the one searching for a 
thousand and one ways to make tomorrow a little 
kinder.

Please let me be there for you, my brave one.

Yours,
Me.

=========================
LETTER 3
=========================

My Dearest,

Hello. It's me again.

I know this is another letter that will never reach you.

There are still so many words I've never managed to say.
They've stayed hidden in my notebooks, my thoughts, 
and countless crumpled pages.

Only now do I realise how much good you brought into my 
life. Yes, there were thorns, but every rose has them.

And somehow, out of everyone I've known, yours were 
the only imperfections I could ever accept—and the only 
ones I ever needed.

Always,
Me.

And somehow, those were the very things I needed most.
