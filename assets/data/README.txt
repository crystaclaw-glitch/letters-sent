=========================
LETTER 1
=========================

My dear,

How are you? I do hope you're keeping well.

You seem rather tired lately. Perhaps it's the way you've been speaking to me, though I may be mistaken. I cannot quite tell whether you're simply exhausted or if something else is weighing on your mind. Whatever it may be, please do tell me.

I only wish to see you feeling a little better.

If you have a thousand troubles, then let me be the one who brings a thousand and one ways to make the burden feel lighter. I cannot promise that I'll be able to solve every problem you face, but I can promise this: I shall remain right here, and I won't let you go through it all on your own.

So please, talk to me, won't you?

You've been so strong for so long, my brave one. Even the strongest souls deserve a place to rest, a shoulder to lean on, and someone willing to listen.

Whenever you're ready, I'll be here.

With all my care,
Yours sincerely

=========================
LETTER 2
=========================

**My Dearest,**

How have you been?

Well... I suppose I already know the answer. You're doing alright, aren't you?

I've been wondering how one is meant to put longing into words. It seems rather impossible. No matter how carefully I choose them, they never quite carry what my heart is trying to say.

After what happened the other day, I realise I shouldn't have said those things to you. I'm truly sorry.

At the time, I genuinely believed it was the right thing for us. I thought perhaps loving you from afar would hurt less than standing close enough to wound each other, whether intentionally or not. It seemed kinder to protect you with distance than to risk causing you more pain.

And yet...

I miss you.

There, I've said it again.

These days, all I can really do is sit quietly and reread the letters we once exchanged—the ones I've kept so carefully all this time. Each page still carries a little piece of you, as though your voice has never quite left the paper.

Sometimes I find myself wondering...

Who gets to see your lovely smile now?

I hope, whoever it is, they realise just how fortunate they are.

Yours, always.

=========================
LETTER 3
=========================

**My Dearest,**

Hello.

It's me again.

Yes, I know this isn't the first letter that will never find its way to you.

Sometimes I wish you could read every word I've never managed to say—the ones tucked away between the pages of my notebook, wandering through my thoughts, or scattered across crumpled sheets of paper I've torn apart more times than I care to admit.

It turns out that saying what we truly feel is rather difficult, doesn't it? Perhaps that's why people so often choose lies instead of honesty. Not because the truth doesn't exist, but because it can be unbearably hard to speak aloud.

Have you realised?

Without you, I haven't quite known how to carry on.

You brought something remarkably good into my life. You made me kinder, calmer... perhaps even a better version of myself. Of course, there were thorns too—painful ones—but I suppose every rose comes with them. And isn't that simply how life works? Every flaw carries a quiet strength somewhere within it.

Out of everyone I've ever met, it was only your imperfections that I never wished to change. They were the only ones I could accept without hesitation.

Perhaps because they came with everything else that made you... you.

And somehow, those were the very things I needed most.
